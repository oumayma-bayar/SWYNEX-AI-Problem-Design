# Labeling Guide

Each email gets exactly ONE category, based on the main problem described.

| Category | Use this label when the email is about... | Example |
|----------|-------------------------------------------|---------|
| Credit Card | Fees, charges, interest, disputes, or fraud on a credit card | "Unknown charge on my card" |
| Mortgage | Home loan payments, servicing, escrow, foreclosure | "Wrong escrow amount" |
| Bank Account | Checking/savings accounts, closures, deposits, overdraft fees | "Account closed without notice" |
| Debt Collection | Calls, letters, or threats from debt collectors | "Collector calls me daily" |
| Student Loan | Student loan payments, servicing, forgiveness | "Payment amount changed" |
| Other | Anything that does not fit the categories above | "General question about services" |

## Rules
1. Label the **main** issue, not secondary ones.
2. If two categories fit, choose the one mentioned first.
3. If the email is unclear or off-topic, label it **Other**.
4. Never keep personal data (names, account numbers, phone numbers).
5. Two people should label 20 emails independently; if they agree on 85% or more, the guide is clear enough.
